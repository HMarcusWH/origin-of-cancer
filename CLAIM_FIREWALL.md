# Claim firewall

### DRIVER_TO_ORIGIN

Driver mutation does not establish malignant onset.

### SELECTION_TO_CAUSATION

Positive selection does not establish cancer causation.

### PATHOLOGY_TO_REGIME

Pathology labels do not define the latent regime by fiat.

### ORDER_TO_CAUSALITY

Temporal order is not causal mediation.

### ANCESTRY_TO_MEDIATION

Ancestry does not establish mechanism.

### SPATIAL_TO_CAUSAL

Spatial colocation does not establish a constitutive niche.

### FIT_TO_EXISTENCE

Good fit does not establish latent-source existence.

### ABLATION_TO_HUMAN

Model ablation does not establish human causal constitution.

### QA_TO_BIOLOGY

Software QA is not biological validation.

### CERTIFICATE_TO_TRUTH

A valid certificate is not empirical truth.

### ONE_ADAPTER_TO_UNIVERSAL

A single adapter does not establish a pan-cancer claim.

### DISCRIMINATION_TO_CALIBRATION

Discrimination is not calibrated prediction.

### MICRO_TO_MACRO_MARKOV

Microscopic Markovity does not establish macroscopic Markovity.

### SAMPLING_IGNORABILITY

Surveillance schedules are not ignorable by default.

### MVCL_TO_ONSET

Full mature MVCL reinforcement is not required as the first-onset definition.

### COHERENCE_TO_ROUTE

Unrelated successful findings do not form a demonstrated end-to-end route.

### FINITE_TO_ASYMPTOTIC

Finite simulation does not prove exact invariance or asymptotic persistence.

### FIT_TO_CONFIRMATION

Construction/calibration data are not independent confirmation.

### THRESHOLD_TRANSFER

OoL/FFBBP thresholds do not transfer to cancer by renaming.

### SOURCE_COUNT_TO_INDEPENDENCE

Duplicate identifiers and shared cohorts are not independent evidence.

### UNKNOWN_TO_FALSE

Unknown or unidentifiable is not a negative experimental finding.

### NEWEST_TO_AUTHORITY

Newer filenames and timestamps do not determine scientific authority.

### CHEMISTRY_TRANSFER

Abiogenesis-specific chemistry and life criteria do not transfer into OoC.

### GOVERNANCE_TO_PERMISSION

Scientific or model admission does not authorize clinical action.

## Enforcement boundary

The validator checks typed records and required evidence/identity bindings. It does
not understand all natural-language claims or automatically judge paper quality.
Human review remains necessary. Engineering PASS with scientific OPEN is a valid
and expected bootstrap outcome. No compensatory overall score is used.
