The external verifier policy pins registry/runtime and frozen thresholds, trusts designated reviewer public keys, and registers evaluator code/symbol/qualification bindings. Data, signatures and metadata cannot protect against compromise of the verifier process or trusted policy itself. This code has regression tests, not an independent penetration/security certification. Unqualified MODEL/SYNTHETIC content cannot be promoted into laboratory certificates; synthetic fixture certificates are explicitly test-only.
