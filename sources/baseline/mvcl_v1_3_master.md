The Minimal Viable Cancer Loop models cancer as a self-sustaining adaptive loop rather than a static list of traits. The loop becomes durable when variation generation, malignant-state switching, protective niche formation, and evolutionary spread reinforce each other faster than tissue governance, immune control, or therapy can collapse the system.

The v1.3 evidence sweep strengthens multiple existing edges: plasticity-to-resistance, metabolism-to-TME/TIME, SV/ecDNA-to-innate immunity, EV/ncRNA-to-metastasis, oncogene-to-organotropism, replication-stress-to-therapeutic vulnerability, and CHIP-to-host/measurement context. None of the new evidence requires a new biological module or a new loop order.

Operationally, the theory becomes a playbook built around four bottlenecks: Deny the Sanctuary, Crash the Replication Engine, Force the Death Decision, and Block the Escape Route. These are strategic categories for research and hypothesis design, not clinical regimens.

# 3. MVCL Core Theory

Cancer becomes durable when a tissue-level governance failure closes into a loop. The loop contains variation, switching, niche protection, and propagation. Once the loop is self-reinforcing, a tumor behaves less like a single broken pathway and more like an adaptive subsystem.

| **One-sentence thesis**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Cancer is a self-sustaining adaptive loop in which variation-generating processes, malignant-state switches, permissive niches, and eco-evolutionary spread reinforce one another until the system becomes resilient to normal tissue control and single-axis therapy. |

- MVCL is not a replacement for the Hallmarks of Cancer; it is a causal compression layer that orders hallmarks into a loop.

- The central object is the edge: a concrete, testable interface between modules.

- The model is useful only if its edges can be measured, controlled, and falsified.

- Interventions should be interpreted as edge and bottleneck hypotheses, not universal treatment recipes.

## 5.1 Somatic Mutation & Clonal Evolution

| **One-line claim**                                                                                                                                 |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Mutational processes create heritable diversity; tissue context, immune pressure, and therapy select clones that drive progression and resistance. |

**MVCL role:** Seed / Spread

Minimal causal chain:

1.  Endogenous/exogenous mutagens and repair errors generate driver and passenger variants.

2.  Early driver alterations alter growth, death, or genome-maintenance cues.

3.  Tissue context and immune pressure filter variants into clonal expansions.

4.  Ongoing mutation/CIN/ecDNA sustains diversification.

5.  Therapy imposes strong selection, expanding resistant subclones.

6.  Clonal architecture becomes branched and spatially heterogeneous.

7.  Recurrent or metastatic disease reseeds the Seed phase with newly selected variants.

| **Field**        | **Content**                                                                                                                                 |
|------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Key invariants   | Variation plus selection; recurrent driver pathways; intratumor heterogeneity; therapy selection.                                           |
| Context switches | Hypermutator vs quiet genomes; pediatric vs adult spectra; neutral drift vs selection; therapy-naive vs treated disease.                    |
| Primary readouts | WES/WGS, mutational signatures, VAF trajectories, ctDNA, single-cell phylogenies, TMB/MSI.                                                  |
| Control levers   | Early detection, surgery/adjuvant timing, targeted therapy against drivers, resistance-aware combinations, adaptive therapy trials.         |
| Key edges        | Feeds CIN/SV/ecDNA, neoantigens/TME-TIME, therapy resistance; receives inputs from exposures, DDR defects, inflammation, microbiome, aging. |
| Falsifier        | If longitudinal sequencing shows mostly neutral drift even under therapy, selection-weighted adaptive strategies lose force.                |
| v1.3 update      | No backbone rebuild; keep neutral drift falsifier and route modeling updates into validation layer.                                         |

14. Therapy can push RS-high tumors beyond viability if toxicity windows are respected.

# 7. Edge System and Edge Schema

# 11. Edge Cases and Context Rules

| **Context**                        | **Rule**                                                                                                              |
|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Profound immunodeficiency          | IO-centric strategies may underperform; emphasize niche disruption and RS/apoptosis where appropriate.                |
| Autoimmune-prone patients          | Heightened IO risk; down-weight IO intensification and rely more on RS/niche/metabolic control when evidence permits. |
| Low-RS / indolent proliferation    | Down-weight aggressive ATR/CHK1/WEE1 strategies; focus on TME, plasticity, and metabolic levers.                      |

| CHIP / ctDNA confound              | Use WBC-matched sequencing where possible; track CH VAF and DDR-mutant CH under therapy pressure.                     |
