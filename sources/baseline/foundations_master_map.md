**OoC THEORY FOUNDATIONS — MASTER MAP & READING ARCHITECTURE**  
Pass 0 working document | Origin of Cancer programme | 26 Sep 2026

PURPOSE  
Build the theoretical substrate required before the Origin-of-Cancer (OoC) kernel is allowed to freeze. This folder is not a bibliography dump. Each theory family must be reconstructed on its own terms, linked to the exact OoC objects it constrains, tested against hostile evidence, and given a claim ceiling.

CURRENT OO C QUESTION  
What can “malignant establishment” mean across colorectal, oesophageal, lung, myeloid, HPV-driven and paediatric systems without becoming either circular or vacuous?

WORKING SEPARATIONS  
• First oncogenic alteration ≠ malignant onset.  
• Positive selection ≠ cancer causation.  
• Malignant-like capability ≠ durable establishment.  
• Temporal order ≠ causal route closure.  
• Phylogenetic ancestry ≠ mechanistic mediation.  
• Precancer pathology ≠ one universal dynamical state.  
• Pathology label ≠ OoC state definition.  
• A fitted attractor / latent state ≠ biological proof.  
• Formal or software verification ≠ empirical validation.  
• Full mature MVCL closure is not required for first malignant onset.

WORKING TRANSITION ARCHITECTURE  
Declared non-malignant boundary (B0)  
→ altered / precursor states  
→ candidate malignant entry  
→ malignant establishment  
→ mature MVCL reinforcement / progression / dissemination

OoC should treat entry and establishment as separate objects. The current hypothesis is that establishment will need some combination of malignant capability, persistence against tissue/host restoring forces, ancestry-continuous route support, and constitutive mechanism evidence, while observability and certificate validity remain separate axes.

FOLDER MAP

00 \- Index & Ontology  
Purpose: master maps, source registry, theory-card template, terminology collisions, cross-theory comparison.

01 \- Classical Carcinogenesis Theories  
Scope: multistage carcinogenesis; mutation accumulation; Knudson two-hit; ordered genetic progression; historical initiation–promotion models; somatic mutation theory.  
Key OoC pressure: Do these theories define origin, or only necessary event accumulation / susceptibility?

02 \- Evolutionary & Ecological Theories  
Scope: clonal evolution; neutral drift; adaptive oncogenesis; evolutionary ecology; public goods/cooperation; polyclonal origin; host-background-dependent selection.  
Key OoC pressure: ancestry and selection are essential, but what distinguishes expanding abnormal clones from malignant establishment?

03 \- Tissue Organization, Promotion & Cell Competition  
Scope: tissue organization field theory; tumour promotion; architecture; cell competition; extrusion; homeostatic constraints; field effects.  
Key OoC pressure: formalize “tissue governance” as measurable restoring/containment mechanisms rather than metaphor.

04 \- Developmental, Cell-of-Origin & Plasticity  
Scope: cell-of-origin; stem/progenitor susceptibility; fetal/developmental origins; differentiation failure; plasticity; lineage-state transitions.  
Key OoC pressure: support cancers whose origin is primarily state/developmental and cannot be represented as decades of mutation accumulation.

05 \- Niche, Immune & Multicellular Establishment  
Scope: niche construction; fibroblast/ECM loops; inflammation; immunosurveillance; immunoediting; stromal dependence; early fibrotic niches.  
Key OoC pressure: determine whether stroma/immune components can be constitutive components of establishment, rather than merely consequences.

06 \- Dynamical Systems, Attractors & Critical Transitions  
Scope: attractor landscapes; bifurcations; critical transitions; tipping points; cancer reversion; state-transition models.  
Key OoC pressure: use regime language only where the biological variables, observability and persistence semantics justify it.

07 \- Mathematical Natural History & Stochastic Establishment  
Scope: Armitage–Doll / MVK / TSCE; branching processes; birth–death; Moran/Wright–Fisher; first passage; multistate models; semi-Markov/HSMM; metastability; quasi-stationarity; stochastic persistence; coarse-graining/lumpability.  
Key OoC pressure: select mathematics by estimand. No universal equation and no Markov assumption by default.

08 \- Lineage, Causality, Observability & Identifiability  
Scope: tumour phylogenetics; single-cell DNA; lineage tracing; spatial ancestry; pseudotime/RNA velocity/OT limits; SCM/mediation; partial identification; observation models.  
Key OoC pressure: identify which origin claims are empirically distinguishable in humans and where only sets of admissible routes can be supported.

09 \- Precancer, Pathology & Boundary Definitions  
Scope: precancer definitions; benign/precursor/in-situ/invasive labels; field cancerization; WHO reference ontology; pathology boundary disagreement.  
Key OoC pressure: use pathology as external concordance, not as a circular definition of the malignant regime.

10 \- Adversarial Theories, Nulls & Falsifiers  
Scope: neutral drift; age-only models; mutation-count models; selection-only models; niche-as-consequence; observation artifacts; regression/non-progression; validation standards; reproducibility.  
Key OoC pressure: every positive origin route must beat serious alternatives and specify what failure changes.

CURRENT HIGH-VALUE EMPIRICAL PRESSURE ON THEORY  
• Normal tissues can carry canonical drivers and positively selected clones without malignant disease: mutation and selection cannot by themselves define OoC.  
• Surrounding mutant epithelium can eliminate emerging tumours: tissue competition belongs in the restoring-force layer.  
• Nascent lesions can construct fibroblast/ECM/immune niches required for persistence: establishment may be multicellular.  
• Polyclonal origins can overcome fitness barriers: founder ontology must allow ensembles and cooperation.  
• Paediatric cancers force developmental / chromatin-state routes rather than adult mutation-first stories.  
• Tissue architecture and host genetic background can change evolutionary trajectories without changing the nominal driver.  
• Spatial and phylogenetic tools improve reconstruction but do not automatically establish causality.  
• Precancer and natural-history states require regression, non-progression, censoring and surveillance processes to be modeled explicitly.

RESEARCH DISCIPLINE  
Reviews map the field; primary studies carry load-bearing mechanistic claims. Preprints remain provisional. Negative findings, non-progressors, regressed/eliminated lesions and competing models are first-class evidence. No source is allowed to silently promote:  
capability → origin,  
selection → causation,  
order → mechanism,  
ancestry → mediation,  
fit → existence,  
ablation → human causal constitution,  
or QA → biological truth.
