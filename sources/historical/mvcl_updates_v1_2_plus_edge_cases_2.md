MVCL v1.2 — Updates, Edge Cases, and
Refinements

This document tracks all updates that extend or nuance the MVCL v1.2 framework and the Universal
Playbook. It is meant as a living addendum you can fold into later versions (v1.3+).


1. Edge Cases to Flag Explicitly

1.1 Immune Context Extremes

       • Profound immunodeficiency (e.g., post-transplant, HIV not fully controlled):
       • IO-centric strategies (A2A, checkpoint inhibitors) may underperform.

       • Emphasize niche disruption (Pillar 1) and RS/apoptosis (Pillars 2–3).

       • Autoimmune-prone patients:

       • Heightened risk with IO intensification.
       • Consider more conservative IO dosing; rely more on RS and niche control.

1.2 Tumors with Low RS / Indolent Proliferation

       • Some tumors do not show strong RS signatures.
       • In such contexts:
       • Down-weight aggressive ATR/CHK1/WEE1 strategies.
       • Focus on TME normalization, plasticity control, and metabolic levers.

1.3 Highly Immunogenic, Low-CIN Tumors

       • Examples: some MSI-H, mismatch repair-deficient cancers.
       • Here, IO alone or IO + TME normalization may be sufficient.
       • RS levers used more cautiously, mainly for combination depth.


2. Refinements to the Four Pillars

2.1 Pillar 1 (Deny the Sanctuary)

       • Add explicit vascular normalization window:
       • Use anti-angiogenic or vessel-normalizing agents at moderate doses.
       • Monitor perfusion (DCE-MRI, perfusion CT) to identify the "access window".

2.2 Pillar 2 (Crash the Replication Engine)

       • Emphasize staggered scheduling to avoid overlapping peak toxicities when combining:
       • ATR/CHK1/WEE1 with high-dose cytotoxics.
       • Suggest 1–2 week on/off windows with careful hematologic monitoring.
