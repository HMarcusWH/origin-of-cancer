3) Context switches

       • Tissue lineage and driver combination dictate pathway dominance (e.g., ER/PI3K in breast; RAS/
      RAF in melanoma).
       • Some genes switch roles (oncogenic vs suppressive) depending on context or non‑coding
       regulation.
       • Extent of replication stress and DDR reliance varies with oncogene load and TSG status.

4) Primary readouts / biomarkers

       • Genomics: hotspot mutations (NGS panels), copy‑number gains/amplifications, fusions
       (RNA‑seq/FISH).
       • Proteins/IHC: HER2, p53, RB, PTEN, phospho‑ERK/AKT; BCL‑2 in selected contexts.
       • Functional: pathway activity signatures; oncogene dependency from DepMap.

5) Anchor datasets / tools

       • TCGA/ICGC driver catalogs; COSMIC; OncoKB for clinical actionability.
       • DepMap CRISPR/RNAi dependency maps; pathway inference tools (PROGENy/GSVA).

6) Control levers (therapies/interventions)

       • Approved: Targeted inhibitors for activated drivers (EGFR/ALK/ROS1/BRAF/HER2/RET/NTRK/KIT/
      KRAS‑G12C); CDK4/6 (RB‑intact); BCL‑2 (venetoclax in heme malignancies).
       • Synthetic lethality / dependencies: PARP in HRD; PRMT5/MAT2A in MTAP loss; ATR/CHK1/
     WEE1 under oncogene‑induced replication stress; proteostasis (HSP90) and oxidative stress
      nodes (to be context‑gated).
       • Combination logic: block bypass pathways; pair with IO when antigenicity permits.

7) Edges (interfaces to other silos)

       • Inputs → Oncogene/TSG: Environmental mutagens & DDR defects create the initiating
       mutations; Epigenetics can silence TSGs or enhance oncogene programs.
       • Outputs →DDR/replication stress (MYC/RAS raise fork stress); Metabolism (MYC/PI3K rewire
       substrate use); Immuno‑oncology (p53 loss & oncogenic signaling affect antigen presentation/
       PD‑L1); CIN/WGD (checkpoint erosion).

8) Ambiguities / controversies

       • True frequency and clinical relevance of context‑dependent role switching of genes/
      non‑coding RNAs.
       • When to prioritize direct driver inhibition vs synthetic‑lethal dependency in multi‑driver
      tumors.
       • Durability of oncogene‑addiction responses vs early combination strategies.
