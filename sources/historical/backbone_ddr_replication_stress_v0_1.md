2) Key invariants

       • Replication stress is pervasive in oncogene‑activated tumors.
       • Specific DDR defects (HRD/MMR‑D/ATM/p53) generate characteristic mutational/structural
       signatures and therapy sensitivities.

3) Context switches

       • Tissue‑specific DDR landscapes (e.g., BRCA/HRD in breast/ovary; ATM alterations in prostate;
     MMR‑D in CRC) shape therapy choices.
       • p53 status, cell‑cycle control, and hypoxia set tolerance to stress and choice of repair pathway.
       • Therapy can raise stress (chemo/radiation), altering dependencies and IO synergy.

4) Primary readouts / biomarkers

       • Replication stress: γH2AX, RPA foci, pCHK1, DNA fiber assays, transcription‑replication collision
       signatures.
       • HRD: genomic scar/LOH scores; RAD51 foci assays.
       • MMR‑D/MSI‑H: PCR/IHC panels; TMB from WES; mutational signatures (e.g., SBS6/15).
       • ctDNA CNAs reflecting stress‑driven SCNAs; ATM/p53 status from NGS.

5) Anchor datasets / tools

       • TCGA/ICGC HRD/MSI/ATM calls; DepMap dependency data (ATR/CHK1/WEE1/POLQ).
       • Tools: SigProfiler; HRD scar callers; MSI PCR/IHC; RAD51 immunofluorescence.
