# Research values and non-negotiable boundaries

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1 safeguards and the user-approved bootstrap architecture.

## Evidence before elegance

Biological evidence outranks solver convenience, a coherent narrative, and the
reputation of an imported framework. A source count, graph edge count, or green
workflow is not a scientific result.

## Explicit uncertainty

Unknown, unmeasured, not tested, not applicable, conflicted, and contradicted are
different states. They must remain visible in text, data, resolver output and review.
No scalar confidence may compensate for a failed necessary gate.

## Rival models and negative-result memory

Present competing theories in their strongest source-supported form. Preserve
failed routes and the exact conditions under which they failed. A successor may
repair a defect without rewriting the predecessor's result.

## Reproducibility and attribution

Every scientific assertion must be traceable to its source or explicitly marked as
an OoC proposal. Keep source identity, source bytes, interpretation, model identity,
execution, and verdict separate. Record AI assistance as drafting/engineering
assistance, not independent review or source evidence.

## Minimal useful formalism

Introduce a concept only when it changes a claim, test, assumption, data requirement,
identification class, or falsifier. Do not force one topology, threshold, founder,
equation, clock, or process family across tissues.

## Research without clinical creep

No patient-specific diagnosis, treatment recommendation, dosing, or clinical action
is authorized by this repository. Do not commit patient identifiers, credentials,
restricted datasets, or redistributed publisher PDFs. Publication licenses are a
separate owner decision; no blanket license is inferred from repository visibility.
