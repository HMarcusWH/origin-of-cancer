snapshots before this deployment. This document records the design inheritance;
it does not copy cosmological or mathematical theorem verdicts into OoC.

contextual DataUse, scoped authority/resolution, negative-result preservation and

partially observed biological routes, separate estimands and applicability, and

record, not a claim of a fresh full audit of either predecessor at this commit.
