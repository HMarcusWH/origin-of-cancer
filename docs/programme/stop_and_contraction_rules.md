# Stop and contraction rules

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

Stop a promotion when the proposed construct is circular, observationally indistinguishable for its intended purpose, unsupported by the declared source boundary, or fails its prespecified test. A failed universal claim may be narrowed only with explicit lost scope, new identity where needed, and retained negative results. Do not rescue every failure by unspecified context.
