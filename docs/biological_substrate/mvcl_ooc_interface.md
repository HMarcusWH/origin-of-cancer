# MVCL / OoC interface

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

MVCL mechanisms may operate before, during or after establishment. OoC must determine stage placement with specific evidence instead of importing mature-cancer behavior into an onset definition. Mechanobiology, EVs, CSC/state and modeling/validation remain overlays. Therapy resistance and metastasis are not universal prerequisites for first malignant onset.
