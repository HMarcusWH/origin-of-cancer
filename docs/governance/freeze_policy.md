# Freeze policy

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

The infrastructure contract is versioned now; the cancer mathematical kernel is not frozen. Every critical requirement needs a reviewed satisfied or explicitly bounded disposition with a precise lost claim/scope. A waiver cannot make a universal claim true. Baseline tests are frozen independently of scientific success.

## Status and traceability

This document is a baseline working synthesis of the supplied programme/method sources, not a completed source appraisal or a new empirical result. See the root documentation-authority contract and source manifest. Scientific promotion requires explicit graph evidence and scoped adjudication.

## Fail-closed freeze adjudication (2026-10 hardening)

The 43 configured Critical RG identifiers are programme gate obligations regardless
of mutable priority labels. Every freeze Requirement and its associated ResearchGap
need separately recorded, evidence-bound Decisions from an ACTIVE_RULE authority
that has RESEARCH_POLICY jurisdiction and permits each decision subject.
Decision.scope denotes governance jurisdiction; disposition_scope denotes the
scientific scope actually adjudicated. For BOUNDED states it must equal the
Requirement/ResearchGap bound_scope, and adjudicated assessments must cover that
same scope. An unresolved or merely SUPERSEDED critical gap never permits freeze.
Synthetic/engineering findings and unadjudicated assessments do not close a
scientific obligation; permitted analytical/formal evidence has model-specific
claim ceilings and cannot establish biological truth by fiat.

### Progress bookkeeping

Scientific RESOLVED/BOUNDED and Requirement SATISFIED/BOUNDED dispositions
are evidence-adjudicated even before a requested kernel freeze. Merely
SUPERSEDED critical gaps remain open blockers until a new closure decision
specifically discharges their obligation. The configured Critical RG set must
match the SHA-locked source programme registry; it is not a mutable priority
count that may be silently rearranged.

## Admissible scientific obligations

A satisfied or bounded ResearchGap/Requirement is checked *before* any future
kernel freeze. Both items declare obligation_domain and obligation_scope
independently of their own Decision. Decision.kind/outcome must agree with
the target's status; an explicit REJECT or KEEP_OPEN is never interpreted as
RESOLVE or SATISFY. Biological-origin obligations require eligible primary
biological support; analytical, numerical and software evidence alone cannot
resolve biological causality. Mathematical/formal evidence requires an exact
scoped result and a verified execution/claim chain rather than a free-text
statement that a proof exists. SUPERSEDED critical gaps remain blockers.

No source paper, explanation, synthetic fixture or result was promoted during
this engineering change. Programme remains GO research / NO-GO kernel freeze.

## Claim-kind-specific evidence

For any resolved/bounded scientific obligation, ResearchGap/Requirement
declare obligation_claim_kind independently of the approving Decision.
BIOLOGICAL CAPABILITY/OBSERVATION, ANCESTRY, PERSISTENCE, ROUTE and
CONSTITUTIVE claims have different permissible evidence ceilings,
experiment designs, and assessment roles. A primary observational result
cannot be relabelled as proof of a causal origin route. All 43 critical
freeze obligations remain OPEN in the authored graph.

## Gap–Requirement equivalence

A configured Critical ResearchGap and its programme Requirement may discharge
one another only when scientific obligation_scope, obligation_domain, and
obligation_claim_kind match, or when a *future explicit independently adjudicated*
coverage certificate authorizes a scientifically legitimate narrower resolution.
No such coverage certificates exist at bootstrap. The 43 currently OPEN
Requirements lack these independently curated obligation fields; the engineering
bootstrap must not populate invented values or assert freeze readiness.

`kernel_frozen=true` requires independent, compatible satisfied/bounded
dispositions for BOTH the critical gap and its linked Requirement. An accepted
Decision is not a substitute for resolving the scientific obligation itself.


### Mathematical obligations and historical provenance

Mathematical obligations cannot be silently satisfied by referring to a
formal proof of the obligation record as though it were a Claim. They must
bind an independent certificate Claim through obligation_claim_ref and
adjudicated evidence of its formal verification. Pre-formalization critical
obligations remain explicitly OPEN until independently curated.

Previously admitted evidence, locators, tests, gates, model specifications,
and their content-bearing source bytes are historical semantic dependencies.
Any change to their scientific meaning requires revision/supersession,
not silent in-place mutation. Reassessment or retraction changes current
admission, not the historical record.

### Append-only freeze and model authorization

A recorded authorization event binds the exact model specification or a declared
critical-gap/Requirement disposition basis. Authorized license and kernel freeze
status are projected from event history and **current** evidence, not obtained by
rewriting a frozen ModelInstance or Programme. The original decisions and basis
remain immutable if subsequent evidence is withdrawn or contested.

Formal proof evidence may discharge only an exact mathematical CERTIFICATE or
MODEL_VALIDITY obligation. Statistical DATA_INDEPENDENCE requires an actual
pair-scoped data audit. Other statistical/methodological obligations remain OPEN
pending a separately defined qualification contract; arbitrary formal certificates
cannot be repurposed into empirical study validation.

### Current authorization vs migration field

When append-only FREEZE_KERNEL/UNFREEZE_KERNEL authorization events exist, their
valid linear tail controls current authorization in **both** directions. A
legacy `kernel_frozen=true` declaration cannot override a subsequent authorized
UNFREEZE. Historical eligibility and contemporary supported dispositions remain
separate; a revoked sole obligation cannot authorize present freeze even if it
was originally accepted. With no events, the legacy field is supported only as
an explicit migration fallback, not as authorization for new freeze approvals.

### External trust policy for formal proof admission

The local `config/trusted_proof_checkers.json` is an untrusted *candidate* policy.
The formal validator deliberately fails closed unless the environment supplies
`OOC_TRUST_POLICY_SHA256`—an exact SHA-256 of the authorized checker policy,
independently provisioned by a trusted operator/protected CI environment, **not**
computed from PR-controlled files during the same run. The policy must explicitly
mark a signer ACTIVE and bind its public key, verifier version and checker SHA-256.
The executing Software must be labeled FORMAL_PROOF_CHECKER with non-placeholder
provenance, and proof receipts use OOC_FORMAL_PROOF_ATTESTATION_V2, signing the
immutable proof Result/Test/Execution/Protocol record contents, statement,
model digest, proof artifact, and exact output/checker hashes. Changed outcomes
or a self-registered key without an external authorization digest cannot promote
mathematical verification. This does not establish that the checker was actually
run or the theorem is true; production key enrollment and verifier execution
still require independent operational governance. A new trust-policy version
requires separate approval and should preserve historical verification context.

## October 27 preemptive repair: statistical contradictions and history

An adjudicated statistical `DATA_INDEPENDENCE` contradiction must meet the
same purpose, scope, active-provenance and data-audit ceiling as positive
independence evidence. A qualified negative assessment contests the current
obligation and removes current freeze eligibility; it does not erase the
historical satisfied/resolved decision. A withdrawn or in-review audit is
not an affirmative statistical contradiction. See
`docs/validation/pr2_scientific_admission_contracts.md`.
