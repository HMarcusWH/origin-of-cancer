# Freeze policy

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

The infrastructure contract is versioned now; the cancer mathematical kernel is not frozen. Every critical requirement needs a reviewed satisfied or explicitly bounded disposition with a precise lost claim/scope. A waiver cannot make a universal claim true. Baseline tests are frozen independently of scientific success.

## Status and traceability

This document is a baseline working synthesis of the supplied programme/method sources, not a completed source appraisal or a new empirical result. See the root documentation-authority contract and source manifest. Scientific promotion requires explicit graph evidence and scoped adjudication.

## Fail-closed freeze adjudication (2026-10 hardening)

The 43 configured Critical RG identifiers are programme gate obligations regardless
of mutable priority labels. Every freeze Requirement and its associated ResearchGap
need separately recorded, evidence-bound Decisions from an ACTIVE_RULE authority
that has RESEARCH_POLICY jurisdiction and permits each decision subject.
Decision.scope denotes governance jurisdiction; disposition_scope denotes the
scientific scope actually adjudicated. For BOUNDED states it must equal the
Requirement/ResearchGap bound_scope, and adjudicated assessments must cover that
same scope. An unresolved or merely SUPERSEDED critical gap never permits freeze.
Synthetic/engineering findings and unadjudicated assessments do not close a
scientific obligation; permitted analytical/formal evidence has model-specific
claim ceilings and cannot establish biological truth by fiat.

### Progress bookkeeping

Scientific RESOLVED/BOUNDED and Requirement SATISFIED/BOUNDED dispositions
are evidence-adjudicated even before a requested kernel freeze. Merely
SUPERSEDED critical gaps remain open blockers until a new closure decision
specifically discharges their obligation. The configured Critical RG set must
match the SHA-locked source programme registry; it is not a mutable priority
count that may be silently rearranged.
