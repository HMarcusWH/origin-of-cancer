# Outcome balance

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

Progressors alone cannot establish prospective discrimination. Include non-progressors, regressed/eliminated lesions and indolent controls where available; record ascertainment, surveillance, competing events, censoring and missing outcomes. An absent control class remains a limitation rather than an invented cohort.
