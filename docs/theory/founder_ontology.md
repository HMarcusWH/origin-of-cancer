# Founder ontology

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

Permit cells, clones, interacting clones, developmental populations and lesion/niche ensembles as candidate origin-bearing objects. Specify the unit, boundary, interactions and ancestry convention. Do not encode a universal monoclonal axiom.
