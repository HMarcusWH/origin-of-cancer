# Claim grammar

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

CAPABILITY, ROUTE, ANCESTRY, PERSISTENCE, CONSTITUTIVE, OBSERVATION and CERTIFICATE are separate kinds. ASSERTION, HYPOTHESIS, CONDITIONAL, REQUIREMENT, FALSIFIER, NON_CLAIM, EXCLUSION and INTERPRETATION are separate modalities. No overall confidence scalar substitutes for these distinctions.
