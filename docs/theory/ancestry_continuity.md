# Ancestry continuity

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

Record genealogical evidence separately from phenotype similarity, spatial proximity, temporal order and causal mediation. A descendant can change genotype/state; continuity does not require an unchanged clone label. Explicit bridge assumptions are required across observation windows.
