# Malignant establishment

> Basis: OoC v0.1.1; MVCL v1.3; the scoped method imports.

The central open problem is a non-circular operational definition that distinguishes transient or contained alterations from established malignant behavior without misclassifying benign persistence. Compare capability, persistence, ancestry and causal support independently. No universal horizon or scalar establishment score is selected.
