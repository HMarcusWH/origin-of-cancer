# Origin of Cancer — provisional research thesis

> Basis: [Origin-of-Cancer Research Programme v0.1.1](sources/baseline/ooc_research_programme_v0_1_1.md), pages 1–10.

> **PRE-FORMALIZATION.** Research programme, not a validated theory or clinical framework.

The source programme asks: when does a declared non-malignant biological system
enter an established malignant regime, through what ancestry-continuous causal
route, and which route components are constitutive under admissible perturbation?

The working sequence is **declared boundary → altered/precursor states → candidate
malignant entry → establishment → mature MVCL reinforcement**. This is a research
organization, not a claim that every cancer follows one temporal sequence.

The candidate contribution is the conjunction of boundary specification, route
closure, ancestry, regime/persistence semantics, intervention-relative constitution,
partial observation, and evidence/certificate separation. Evolution, attractors,
multistage carcinogenesis and microenvironmental influence are not claimed as novel.
